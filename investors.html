Why Chicagoland

An investor's case for the metro.

Chicago continues to offer more space and lower entry prices than coastal markets, while median rents around $1,967 across the metro support healthy cash flow in the right neighborhoods. Demand stays strong, and the market's steady mid-single-digit appreciation makes for a more predictable long-term hold than the boom-bust coasts.

The opportunity is uneven, though — it lives at the neighborhood and property level. The difference between a deal that cash-flows and one that drains you comes down to running real numbers: rents, taxes (Cook County matters), insurance, vacancy, and capex. That's where an agent who knows the local figures earns their keep.

Whether you're after a first rental, a value-add rehab, or a small multi-unit, I help you find properties that work on paper before you commit.
