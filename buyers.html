The Market Right Now

What Chicagoland buyers face in 2026.

The Chicago metro is competitive but more manageable than the pandemic years. The median sale price across the metro sits around $365,000, with steady year-over-year appreciation in the mid-single digits rather than the double-digit spikes of the early 2020s.

Inventory remains tight, which keeps well-priced, move-in-ready homes moving quickly — sometimes with multiple offers — while homes needing work or priced too high are sitting longer. Mortgage rates have hovered around 6%, so getting pre-approved and acting decisively on the right home matters more than ever.

The takeaway: buyers who are prepared, realistic on price, and quick to move have real opportunities. That preparation is exactly what I help with.
