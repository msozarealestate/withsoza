The Market Right Now

Why 2026 favors prepared sellers.

Tight inventory across Chicagoland continues to support sellers. In sought-after communities with limited supply, well-presented, move-in-ready homes are selling quickly and often at or near asking price — in the hottest pockets, a meaningful share sell above list.

But the market has become more discerning. Homes that are overpriced or need obvious work are sitting longer and seeing more price cuts than a year or two ago. Pricing strategy and presentation are everything. Get those right and you command the market; get them wrong and you chase it down.

My job is to position your home so it lands in front of the right buyers, priced to attract competition rather than to sit.
